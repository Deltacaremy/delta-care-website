    How should I store the products?

    Store unmixed vials in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. After reconstitution, keep them refrigerated. Capsules should be kept in a cool, dry place.

    What is a COA?

    A Certificate of Analysis (COA) is a lab report that confirms a product's identity and purity. Products with a "COA Verified" badge include one. Tap "View COA" on the product page to see it.
